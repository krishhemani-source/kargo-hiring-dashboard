KARGO HIRING RUBRIC - PM & SENIOR PM
Derived from 8 past hires (Exceeds: Rohan, Sunita, Aditya, Meghna, Lavanya | Meets: Vikram, Rahul | Below: Preetham)

Key insight: Spec fit did not predict success. Vikram (3 yrs PM, XLRI MBA, Product School CPO)
= Meets. Lavanya (2 yrs PM) = Exceeds.

======================================================================
PATTERNS IN HIGH-RATED HIRES NOT IN THE JDs
======================================================================

1. Did the work before building for it (5/5 Exceeds, 0/3 others)
   All high performers spent years in hands-on freight operations before their current
   function: Rohan (CHA documentation, JNPT), Sunita (forwarder documentation/customs),
   Aditya (port services, Nhava Sheva), Meghna (forwarder documentation),
   Lavanya (carrier ops, Mahindra Logistics).
   JDs only ask for "curiosity" (PM) or "familiarity" as an advantage (SPM),
   not having done the job.

2. Owned breakages beyond their role (5/5 vs 0/3)
   Meghna: overnight customs hold resolved before client knew. Sunita: weekend workflow
   redesign after vendor format change. Aditya: converted 3 accounts during a competitor's
   port congestion failure. Lavanya: wrote and closed out outage post-mortem. Rohan: caught
   a failing data vendor and led migration.
   Preetham's incident work was an assigned on-call rotation; Vikram and Rahul show only wins.

3. Unprompted fixes adopted by the people doing the work
   Rohan's Excel tracker (12-person ops team in 2 weeks), Sunita's SOPs (adopted in 60 days),
   Meghna's onboarding checklist (team standard), Lavanya's visibility dashboard (spread to
   2 other regional teams).
   Weakest discriminator: Vikram and Rahul also had adopted artifacts, but for their own
   function, not frontline operators.

Caveat: n=8, only 2 PM hires. Treat these as hypotheses.

======================================================================
PM RUBRIC  (score each criterion 1-5, multiply by weight)
======================================================================

1. Hands-on operations experience ............................ 30%
   1 = Knows domain only via calls/reading
   5 = 2+ yrs personally doing freight/logistics/ops work

2. Shipping & kill judgment .................................. 20%
   1 = Feature lists, no outcomes
   5 = Shipped in short cycles, killed features on data, explains why

3. Unprompted fix that got adopted ........................... 20%
   1 = Only assigned work
   5 = Built tool/process unasked; peers/users adopted fast

4. Owns breakages ............................................ 15%
   1 = No incidents, or escalated only
   5 = Fixed a live failure outside their remit and documented it

5. Works without structure ................................... 15%
   1 = Joined an established PM org with a playbook
   5 = Sole owner; built own rhythms and templates

   TOTAL ..................................................... 100%

======================================================================
SENIOR PM RUBRIC  (score each criterion 1-5, multiply by weight)
======================================================================

1. Platform/integration ownership & hard calls ............... 25%
   1 = Features only; senior PM made calls
   5 = Owned integration/data layer; made build/configure/avoid calls

2. Hands-on operations experience ............................ 25%
   1 = Domain known from a distance
   5 = Personally did docs/customs/carrier/port work

3. Reliability & breakage ownership .......................... 15%
   1 = No incidents shown
   5 = Led vendor migrations, outages, data-quality fixes to closure

4. Set standards others adopted .............................. 15%
   1 = Followed existing process
   5 = Created practices other teams took up

5. Commercial / customer impact .............................. 10%
   1 = No link to revenue or customers
   5 = Integration they owned unblocked a deal or opened a segment

6. Early-stage tenure (5-8 yrs) .............................. 10%
   1 = Only large, structured companies
   5 = Operated where rules weren't written; no layer above

   TOTAL ..................................................... 100%

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Gate (pass/fail, not weighted): Mumbai-based or willing to relocate; in-office.

Scoring: Weighted score (%) = sum(score x weight) / 5
Sanity check on past PM hires (by eye): Lavanya ~97%, Vikram ~43%, which matches
their ratings. Partly circular, since the patterns came from these same hires.
